A connected foundation for modern care teams. MediCore is a browser-based healthcare management prototype focused on clean workflows across patients, clinicians and operations.

About MediCore

Designed as a connected healthcare operating layer

MediCore is a frontend product prototype focused on showing how patient-facing and operational workflows can work together without a backend dependency.

Principle

Patient-first experience

The interface keeps appointments, clinical history, prescriptions and reports easy to locate.
